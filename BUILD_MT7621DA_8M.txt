u-boot-mt7621.bin contains TPL/SPL/compressed U-Boot. The partition is 192KiB.
Do not overwrite env at 0x30000 or Factory at 0x40000. Firmware starts 0x50000.
This build intentionally targets only the 8MiB board, not a universal 16MiB DT.

Hold RESET at power-on. GPIO18 must stay low for 500ms; RUN blinks while
waiting for release (up to 10 seconds). Recovery is on LAN0..3 only:
PC 192.168.1.2/24, browser http://192.168.1.1/ . WAN PHY4 is isolated.
Autoboot failure also enters Web recovery. Requires board verification.

Recovery validates actual firmware size 0x7b0000, FIT hashes and the exact
8MiB board compatible/embedded firmware reg, plus a complete SquashFS.
MIPS Linux legacy uImage+SquashFS is bounded and CRC-checked; it has no
reliable DT identity check. Third-party legacy firmware needs explicit prior
verification of board, DDR, flash layout and port mapping before installation.
No arbitrary third-party firmware compatibility is claimed.
HTTP body limit includes bounded multipart overhead; Flash writes/erases
remain bounded by the firmware partition. Readback verification is enabled.
These are corruption/layout checks, not signed firmware authentication.
